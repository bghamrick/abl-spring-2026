Before you install

A quick note before you start.

The ABL system works by handing instructions to your own AI assistant, which then sets things up on your own computer. ABL doesn't run the install for you — you (with Alfred and your AI team) are in the driver's seat.

A few honest things to know:

  • AI is powerful but not perfect. AI-assisted setup can vary from run to run. We can't guarantee a specific result on your specific machine.
  • It's your environment. You're responsible for understanding what you're installing, for backing up your files first, and for keeping your own passwords, API keys, and tokens safe.
  • Use at your own risk. ABL provides these materials “as is,” with no warranty. We're not liable for anything that breaks, slows down, gets lost, or goes wrong on your system as a result of installing or running this material.

If you're not comfortable with what a step does, pause and ask — Alfred can explain it before you proceed. When in doubt, don't run it.
