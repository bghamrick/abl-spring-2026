Week 0 · May 23 · Start Here

What to do before Saturday at 1 PM ET.

Two things. Plan on about 40 minutes total. Don't rush either of them — both shape how Week 1 lands.

  1. Install your ABL folder (~15 minutes)

    The folder is your private workspace. It's just plain-text files and an AI assistant named Alfred who knows how to read them. I sent you a Google Drive link in the access email — download the zip, unzip into your Documents folder, and rename the folder to ABL.

    Full install steps (with macOS and Windows commands) live in the install guide in your inbox. If anything breaks, message me — don't fight the tool.

  2. Do the mental inventory (~20 minutes)

    Take a pen and a piece of paper — not a screen. Spend fifteen quiet minutes writing three columns:

    1. What I'm currently doing  ·  2. What I should be doing  ·  3. What I want to be doing.

    That paper stays yours — Alfred won't read it. But it's the starting point for the first conversation he and you have on Saturday. Bring it with you to the Zoom.

  3. Show up Saturday at 1 PM ET

    We meet on the same Zoom link every Saturday: us02web.zoom.us/s/3047605686. Bring your laptop, the install done, and the mental inventory page. We'll do the rest together.

If something breaks

Email me directly at bg@bghamrick.com. The install is the only part of this where I want you to ask for help before Saturday. Everything else can wait until we're live.
